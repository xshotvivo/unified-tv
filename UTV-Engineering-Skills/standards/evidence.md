# Evidence and claim standard

- A / verified: authoritative source or directly inspected artifact.
- B / measured: reproduced measurement with method and conditions.
- C / inference: reasoned conclusion with assumptions.
- D / target: desired threshold, not measured.
- Unknown: not established.

For B evidence record device/model, firmware/build, app version, fixture, network/environment, duration, repetitions, raw artifact and date. A single successful run does not establish reliability. Record counter-evidence and what would falsify an inference.
